# 05 The other four scoreboards

Status: ready-for-agent
Type: task
Blocked by: 01

Collectors into the same store, each with its own definition of proven (repeat wins) and its own
pairs: Stack Exchange (top-voted answer beside a low-voted answer to the same question; authors
with many top answers), Reddit and Hacker News (same idea), Wikipedia (featured or good articles
beside ordinary articles of similar topic and length), newsletters and essays (choose the source
and say what its score is), ads and split tests (choose the source; collect only, analysed in the
second pass). Lift the Reddit collector from `apps/research` on `research/reel-craft`. **Pilot bound:** about 4,000 pages and 20 pairs across these four. For each
source record in the README: access route, limits, what the score means. Commenters and private
people are never named in findings; publishers are.
