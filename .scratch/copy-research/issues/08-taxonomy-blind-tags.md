# 08 Taxonomy and blind tags

Status: ready-for-agent
Type: research
Blocked by: 02, 03, 05, 07

Readers never know which page of a pair won. Two agents each write a taxonomy from separate mixed
samples, seeded with the canon's claims; merge as in the hook study. Tag the pilot's 50 pairs
(spread over the scoreboards), every page by two different agents. Record per-tag agreement; a tag
below about 70% is unreliable. Run through `job`, at most 10 readers at once, stopping at three
quarters of the Claude 5-hour window, never in the hours before the weekly SEO run. Record tokens
and minutes per page: the pilot report needs them.
