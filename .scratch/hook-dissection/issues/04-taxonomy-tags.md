# 04 Taxonomy and blind tags

Status: ready-for-agent
Type: research
Blocked by: 03

Agents never see outlier status. Tagging set: each account's 6 strongest real outliers plus their
date twins (921 reels).

1. **Two independent taxonomies:** two agents, each on its own shuffled mixed sample of ~150 reels
   (no overlap). Merge: a mechanism both lists find is kept; one found by only one list is checked
   against its example reels before it stays.
2. **Tagging:** parallel agents, batches of ~35, tag every reel across the four layers (spoken,
   on-screen text, first frame and pace, caption/CTA) with the merged list.
3. **Agreement check:** ~10% of reels (shuffled into other batches) are tagged twice by different
   agents. Per-mechanism agreement is recorded; a mechanism below ~70% agreement is reported as
   unreliable, not as a finding.
4. **Revision:** proposed new mechanisms are reviewed once; real ones join the list and only the
   reels that could carry them are re-tagged.
