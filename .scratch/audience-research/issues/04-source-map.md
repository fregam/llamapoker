# 04 Source map (any language, three tiers)

Status: ready-for-agent
Type: research
Blocked by: 01

The unit is one community or comment section; the universe is ~1,000–2,000 candidates.

1. **Find everything:** 6–8 parallel agents (one per platform family × language group, lighter effort)
   plus API searches and directories. Each writes candidates with their numbers (followers, posts a
   week, comments a post, last activity, language, access, tool route) to a file.
2. **Score in code:** a formula ranks all candidates (activity, recency, comment density, reachability).
3. **AI judges the top ~200:** ~8 parallel agents, ~25 sources each. Sample each source and score the
   share of recreational players, own-voice density and tone, with a one-line reason.

Output: ranked `sources.json`, plus a short note to the founder (veto possible, sign-off not needed).
Deep runs (07) take the top 30–60.
