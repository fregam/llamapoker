## Growth mechanics (used in 002 and 003)

- **Hook under one second:** the question in Bodoni plus the cards, before anything moves.
- **Reading line:** a brass hairline fills across the bottom for the whole reel, so viewers see
  how little is left and stay.
- **Share line** aimed at one specific friend ("Send this to the friend who chases every gutshot").
- **Comment bait** at the end: a question with "wrong answers in the comments".
- **Seamless loop:** the outro's engraving is the hook's engraving, so the replay doesn't jump.
- **Typeset captions:** Inter on the ink, the key word in Bodoni italic with a hairline drawing
  itself under it. Readable on mute, and not the boxed-subtitle look of every other Remotion reel.
