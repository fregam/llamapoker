# Calendar

The publishing rhythm proposed in `docs/plan-2026-11-11.md` (Phase 1), to hold from the week the
first reel is posted. Fifteen minutes a day of approvals plus one 90-minute batch on Sunday.
Each row links to the piece's file once it exists; status: draft → approved → scheduled → published.

| Day | Piece | Channel | Where it leads |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sunday (batch, 90 min) | Voice, review and render the week's two reels; approve the week's queue | — | — |
| Tuesday 18:00 | Reel | Instagram, then Shorts and TikTok the same day | bio link `/go/<channel>` → guide or Table → letter |
| Thursday 18:00 | The letter (short: one spot, one drill, one thing) | Beehiiv | `/table` or a guide, tagged `utm_source=letter` |
| Friday 18:00 | Reel | Instagram, then Shorts and TikTok | as Tuesday |
| Every second week | One Learn guide from the research brief | llamapoker.com | the letter form in its footer |
| One a week | The distribution bet: a helpful answer in a community, hand-posted | r/poker or similar | a guide, never a bare link |
