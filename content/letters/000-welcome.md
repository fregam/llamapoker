Good evening. Pull up a chair.

You're on the LlamaPoker letter. Once a week you'll get one spot to think through, one drill to
practise and one thing worth knowing about the game. Five minutes, and your next session goes a
little better. Nothing else, and you can leave with one click.

## Start with this one

You hold **A♥ 5♥**. Two players see the flop with you: **K♥ 9♥ 2♣**. The first player bets 50
into 100. Most players call. The read says **raise**, and the reason is a number: against two
opponents you have about **54% equity** and the call only needs **25%**. A raise wins the pot now
some of the time, and makes the best flush about a third of the time when it doesn't.

[Put the spot on the table](https://llamapoker.com/table?utm_source=letter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome)
and change the ranges. Watch the number move.

## Then two minutes of outs

Count the cards that make your hand, multiply by four on the flop or two on the turn, and you
have your chance of hitting. The trainer deals you boards against a clock until it sticks.
[Open the outs drill](https://llamapoker.com/trainer?utm_source=letter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome)

## One thing

LlamaPoker is for studying between sessions, never for use during real-money play. Most sites
ban that, and you'd learn nothing from it anyway. 18+, and play with money you can afford to lose.

Reply with a hand you weren't sure about. Every reply gets read, and the best ones become the
spot of the week.

*Until the next hand,*
The Gentleman
