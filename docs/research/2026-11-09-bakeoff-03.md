# Bake-off 03

**Question:** can motion authored in Blender with a simple-shape mannequin drive an open video
model (compute only, no paid API) and keep the brand look?

**Answer: yes.** Wan Animate 2 followed every beat of the guide and held the cream-on-black
engraving in every frame.

## Pipeline that worked

1. **Guide:** `apps/blender/shots/bakeoff-03/s01_bow-tie.py` on `gentleman_mannequin_v01.blend`.
   5 s, 24 fps, 9:16. Clay + depth passes rendered locally in about a minute.
2. **Look:** one on-brand keyframe from FLUX 3 (Higgsfield, 3 credits) with library references
   (turnaround d0c5be13, poses ed9c7478, loft plate f43a005b). It doesn't need to line up with the
   guide for Wan Animate.
3. **Render:** Comfy `video_wan_animate2`: reference image = keyframe, driving video = clay guide.
   GPU time only. Overrides needed: `291:80` and `672:245` `chroma_subsampling: auto` (the
   template's `none` fails validation); prompts on `672:582` (character/background) and
   `672:585` (action).

Result: `outputs/s01_wan-animate-2_mannequin-guide.mp4` (480×848). Strip:
`outputs/s01_wan-animate-2_strip.png`.

## Findings

- Beats land as authored: looks off left → hoof to bow tie → hoof down → turn to the lens →
  push-in. This is the precision we were missing with prompt-only models.
- Simple shapes are the right guide: no limb or face artefacts were copied.
- **Fix:** on the turn to camera the head tilts up. Keep the head level in the shot script
  (counter-rotate `head` X while turning).
- **Fix:** output is 480p. Next: SeedVR2 upscale to 1080×1920 + check whether the template can
  run at 720p.
- FLUX won't re-frame a keyframe to match a blocking render; it copies the reference image's
  layout instead. When a model needs an aligned first frame (LTX IC-LoRA depth), use Wan
  Animate's first frame.

## Cost

Higgsfield: 6 credits (keyframes) + 6 (failed alignment attempt). Comfy: GPU time only for Wan Animate.
