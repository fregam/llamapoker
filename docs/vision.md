# Vision

**LlamaPoker becomes a poker media brand, and the free tool is how people find it.**

1. **The tool (live):** a free odds calculator, trainer and beginner guides at llamapoker.com.
   It ranks in search for what beginners type ("poker odds calculator", "online poker helper",
   "poker positions") and turns visitors into newsletter subscribers.
2. **The newsletter:** the LlamaPoker letter (Beehiiv): drills, strategy, news. It's the
   audience we own.
3. **Content engine:** agents that research what players talk about (Reddit, X/Twitter,
   tournament results), draft articles, handle SEO and outreach, and produce social posts and
   short videos (Higgsfield). A human approves what gets published.
4. **Press:** real coverage at tournaments: interviews, reports, photos.
5. **Commerce:** merch and products once the audience and the brand are strong.

**Principles:** useful first (every piece teaches something), honest (no real-time-assistance
marketing, no gambling glamour), one consistent brand (`brand/README.md`).
